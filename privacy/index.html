Golf Trek privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Golf Trek is a golf scoring app. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the rights you have under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

The responsible party is EPITOME SOFTWARE (PTY) LTD, South Africa, the publisher of Golf Trek on the App Store. Our Information Officer is Peet van Biljon. For anything about your personal information, write to privacy@golftrek.app.

What we collect, and why

InformationWhy we need it
Your email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash)To create your account, sign you in, and send the confirmation code.
Your first name, last name and display nameTo show who you are on scorecards, in groups and to golfers you play with.
Your gender (or "prefer not to say") and your handicap indexTo pick the right tee ratings and work out your playing handicap.
Your rounds, hole scores, games, groups and the golfers you followThis is what the app does: scoring, results and playing together.
A player you add as a guest: their names, gender, handicap index and, if you give it, their emailSo your group can score their card. Their email is used only to let them claim the round if they sign up.

Your location stays on your phone. When you allow it, Golf Trek uses your phone's location to open on the course nearest you and sort courses by distance. Your position is never sent to us or saved.

Golf Trek has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell your information or use it to profile you, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Who we share it with

  • Other golfers see what the app shows them: your display name and handicap on cards and games you play together, and in groups you belong to. Your profile is private unless you make it public. Your email is never shown to other golfers.
  • Supabase hosts Golf Trek's database and sign-in, as our operator under POPIA, on servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Resend delivers the app's emails, such as your sign-up code.
  • Apple distributes the app. Apple's own privacy policy covers what Apple collects.

Because our servers are in the European Union, your information is transferred out of South Africa (POPIA section 72). The EU's General Data Protection Regulation gives it protection at least as strong as POPIA's, and our providers are bound by agreements to keep it secure and use it only for Golf Trek.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, it is removed at once, as described below. A guest's details are kept only while the rounds they were added to are kept.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Profile → Delete account, then type your email to confirm. It happens at once and cannot be undone. We remove your sign-in, your email, your names, your gender, your handicap history, the golfers you follow and who follow you, your group memberships, your goals and the rounds you played alone.

Your scores on cards you played with other golfers stay, with your name replaced by "Deleted golfer", so their results and leaderboards do not change. This kept information no longer identifies you. If you prefer, write to privacy@golftrek.app and we will delete your account for you.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we process it. You can change your names in the app (Profile → Edit profile) and delete your account yourself. For anything else, write to privacy@golftrek.app, and we will answer within 30 days.

If you are not happy with our answer, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

Security

Your information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your session is stored in your phone's Keychain. On our servers, each golfer can reach only what the app's rules allow them to see.

Children

Golf Trek is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. A change to what we collect or who we share it with will be shown in the app before it takes effect.
